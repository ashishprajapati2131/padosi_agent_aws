How publishing works
  • Press Publish on a draft to make it the page at /privacy/. The previous live policy becomes a draft.
  • On the add and edit forms, Publish saves and goes live. Save as draft keeps the current live policy.
  • Deleting the live policy publishes the newest one that is left. If none are left, the built-in policy is shown so the public link never goes blank.
  • Scripts in the policy text are removed when you save.
