Delete Your Account

Request permanent deletion of your Padosi Agent App account and all associated personal data.

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.
Once your account is deleted, you will lose access to all your data, leads, properties, and transaction history.
What Data Gets Deleted
Data Category Action
Profile Information (name, email, phone, photo) Deleted
Property Listings & Media Deleted
Leads & Contact History Deleted
Transaction & Deal Records Deleted
Saved Searches & Preferences Deleted
Push Notification Tokens Deleted
Authentication Credentials Deleted
Anonymized Analytics (non-personal) Retained

* All data is deleted after one month. We cannot remove everything immediately so that an accidental deletion can still be reviewed and restored if needed within that period.

Delete from the App

You can delete your account directly from the Padosi Agent App:

  1. 1

    Open Settings

    Open the Padosi Agent App and navigate to Profile → Settings

  2. 2

    Go to Account Section

    Scroll down and tap on Account & Security

  3. 3

    Tap Delete Account

    Tap on Delete My Account at the bottom of the page

  4. 4

    Verify Your Identity

    Re-enter your account password to confirm it is you

  5. 5

    Confirm Deletion

    Confirm to lock your account immediately. After 1 month, all your data will be permanently deleted.
