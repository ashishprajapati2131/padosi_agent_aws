1. LEGAL BASIS

This Privacy Policy complies with:

  • Information Technology Act, 2000

2. INFORMATION COLLECTED

A. Free sign-up

Creating a free account collects only what is needed to open and contact that account:

  • Name — to identify the account holder
  • Mobile number — to verify the account and send service messages
  • Email address — to sign in, send account notices, and recover access
  • Pincode — to place the account in a service area and show relevant local results

A pincode is an area code. It is not a precise GPS location unless you later choose to share your device location.

B. Other personal data

  • PAN, licences, and certifications, when you add them for verification
  • Business details and profile content you choose to publish

C. Technical Data

  • IP address
  • Device details
  • Cookies
  • Usage analytics

D. Social Media Manager

If you use Social Media Manager, we process the content you create or ask us to send:

  • Messages
  • Posts
  • Photos and videos

On your instruction, that content is sent to these services so they can publish, schedule, or deliver it:

  • Artha
  • Meta (including Facebook and Instagram)
  • Google
  • X
  • Telegram

We send only the content and the account details required to deliver what you asked for. After it reaches those services, they handle it under their own privacy policies. You must have the right to share the messages, posts, photos, and videos you submit.

E. AI writing

If you use AI writing, we process:

  • The text you enter, including prompts, notes, and drafts
  • The text the tool generates for you

That text may be sent to an AI service provider solely to produce the draft you requested. Do not enter passwords, card numbers, or another person’s sensitive information. Review every draft before you publish or send it. AI writing can be incomplete or incorrect, and you remain responsible for what you use.

F. Payment Data

Handled only by third-party gateways. We do not store card data.
