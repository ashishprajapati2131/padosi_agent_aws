Data Privacy & Account Deletion

Account Termination & Data Purge Request

We respect your right to privacy and control over your personal data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act. Review our selective data destruction and retention policy below before submitting your verified request.

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon administrative execution of your request, the following personal data is permanently destroyed and unrecoverable:

  • Account Profile & KYC: Permanent Account Number (PAN), residential & business address, and KYC documents (IRDAI, AMFI, and family licenses).
  • Media & Images: Profile photos, portfolio samples, achievement gallery, and personalized marketing cards.
  • Leads & Client PII: Contact notes, client phone/email, and customer inquiry histories are permanently purged.
  • Portfolio & Business Stats: Client base count, claims settled amounts, performance metrics, and registered insurance segments.
  • Location & Device Data: Geolocation coordinates, serviceable pincodes, push notification device tokens (FCM), and active session tokens.

Data Retained for Legal Compliance

As mandated by Indian regulatory and tax authorities, specific non-marketing records must be preserved:

  • User Identity Records: Full name, registered phone number, and email ID are retained strictly for future user identification, anti-fraud safeguards, and audit logs.
  • Tax & GST Invoices: Payment tax receipts and GST filings are legally retained for statutory financial audits (mandatory 6–8 years under Indian GST and Income Tax laws).
  • Payment Transactions: Bank transaction IDs, Razorpay order identifiers, and settlement audits to resolve chargebacks and fraud inquiries.
  • Deletion Audit Trail: A timestamped record showing that your deletion request was executed, ensuring regulatory compliance under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.
